HELLO, I’M JOSH

A builder’s curiosity.
A notebook’s worth of lessons.

I explore the space where data, cloud platforms, and AI meet—and build things to understand it better.

My projects here span Azure, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, and AI agents. I’m interested in the practical details: how the pieces connect, what breaks outside a demo, and which decisions make a solution easier to use.

That curiosity also takes me into interactive visualization, including maps and race stories drawn from West Virginia cross-country results.

Why “Notes by Josh”?

This site began as a place to record what I was learning. It’s grown into a home for the work itself, with the notebook still close by. You’ll find finished experiments alongside troubleshooting notes and study guides.

Building with AI

AI is both a subject I explore and a tool I use. In my project write-ups, I describe where it helped—from wrangling data to building visualizations—and share the details behind the result.
